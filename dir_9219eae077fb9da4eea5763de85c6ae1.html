Detailed Description

Test 8

A temporal policy, executed end to end: the corridor mission planned with durations.

With durations, the corridor's best policy is not the shortest one. Sending both robots to look at once takes 30 s (drive) + 5 s (look) = 35 s; one robot looking and telling the other takes 30 + 5 + 1 = 36 s, though it is one step shorter. The planner (EPISTEMIC.temporal: true) returns the 35 s policy, a temporal one: the two drives overlap, and so do the two looks, because their updates commute in every state the running set can pass through.

Epistemic

  • Temporal planning with the action mapping's durations
  • A temporal plansys2_msgs/Plan (temporal, starts_after, given_*)
  • EpistemicBTBuilder rendering it for event-driven dispatch
  • WaitForEnds, CheckGiven, MarkDone gating every item
  • Two sensing actions running at once against one epistemic state
  • CheckEpistemicGoal at the end

What it checks

Both robots drive and both look, nobody reports, the mission succeeds, and the executor's own timestamps show the drives overlapping and the looks overlapping. It runs twice, once per corridor state.

A performer serves one request at a time, so there are two performers for each action that overlaps with itself. The classical half is test 5's PDDL.
